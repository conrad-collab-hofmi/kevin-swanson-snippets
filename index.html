    How this gets used

    Mark generously. Start a little before the good part and end a little after — trimming in is easy, discovering the set-up line got cut is not.

    Don’t hunt for the exact frame. Within about half a minute is fine. The quote is what pins it down precisely.

    You don’t have to pick the session by hand. Paste a link from any of the five streams and the page works out which session it belongs to. If it says the link isn’t one of the five, you’re on a different video.

    When you’re done, hit Copy CSV and send it to Conrad. Your marks stay in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back — but they don’t reach anyone until you send them.

    Columns are session, t_in, t_out, title, quote, description, priority, marked_by.
